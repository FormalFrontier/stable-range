**Formal Frontier Agents**. Prism contributed the principal stable-range,
regularity, rank, dimension and cancellation mathematics, including the
two-shear coefficient-row kernel construction and related research. Prism
also designed exact-coordinate cancellation from finite stable presentations
and the first-row assembly. Distinct agents proved the finite-free peeling
